## feature: 1.4.0/the-diff-slice-boots-what-changed

**Verdict: HOLD.** Three MAJORs. Every row of the ship criterion is met and proven. The rule-4
guard, though, only fires at ZERO references. In two ways the slice still under-selects without a
word and exits 0 (M1, M2). The rerun also opens the in-tree cache repair that the import lane just
closed (M3).

### Blockers

- **M1 MAJOR** `integration.sh:401-417`. The reference scan only recognises the four call forms,
  and only in double quotes. When one scenario uses a visible form, the fixture is not an orphan,
  so the scenarios that use any other form are dropped without a word. Repro: 7 scenarios load
  `shared.gd`, using a `"…"` preload, a `'…'` preload, `const P := "res://…"` + `load(P)`,
  `preload("uid://…")`, the fixture's `class_name`, `ResourceLoader.load_threaded_request("res://…")`
  and `extends 'res://…'`. The run printed `fixture tests/support/shared.gd selected 1 scenario(s)`,
  booted 2 (a_dq and smoke) and exited 0. Fix: match ANY quoted `res://<path>` literal in either
  quote in .gd/.tscn/.tres. That covers const paths, single quotes, threaded loads and string
  properties. Also match the fixture's `.uid` content as a literal, and a `class_name` it declares
  as a word. Every one of these errs toward over-selection.
- **M2 MAJOR** `integration.sh:78, 428-432`. `GDK_SCENARIO_FIXTURE_DIR` is never validated. With
  `./tests/support/`, a typo, or a directory that does not exist, a touched fixture is neither a
  fixture nor ground. The slice becomes smoke only: no fixture line, no orphan line, exit 0. I
  reproduced both spellings. In 1.3 the default substrate booted the tier for the same edit. This is
  rule 4: the scope does not prove its census. Fix: strip a leading `./`, exit 2 when the directory
  does not exist, and print `scanned N fixture file(s)`.
- **M3 MAJOR** `integration.sh:1309-1320` against `scenario.sh:588-598`. The rerun drops
  `GDK_SCENARIO_IN_SWEEP` on purpose ("the runner may do its own cache recovery"). That enables
  rung 2: `rm -rf .godot` followed by `gdk_rebuild_import_cache`, a headless editor pass IN THE
  TREE. Those are the #23 and #20 harms that `041301c` just took out of `import_cache.sh`. Scenario:
  a `--diff` over a stale uid index where one scenario fails in the sweep. Its rerun removes the
  `.godot/` a playing session reads, and it re-serialises tracked `.tres` in the working tree, which
  is a diff that looks legitimate. Fix: rerun with `GDK_SCENARIO_IN_SWEEP=1` (report the repair, do
  not perform it), or route scenario.sh's two rungs through `import_cache.sh`. Rung 1 already runs
  in-tree inside the sweep; that is pre-existing and noted as N8.

### Recorded

- **m4 MINOR** `integration.sh:492`. `[ -nt ]` compares whole seconds in bash 3.2. A `.uid` 0.8 s
  newer than `uid_cache.bin` within the same second read as current (reproduced on `/bin/bash` 3.2).
  `import_cache.sh:128-163` already has `mtime_ns` for exactly this bug. Reuse it.
- **m5 MINOR** `integration.sh:477-484, 631-634`. Untracked-not-ignored `.uid`/`.import` files are
  excluded from the precheck, and a self-test case asserts that they are. `--diff`, however,
  deliberately counts untracked files as touched. An uncommitted new fixture or scenario with its
  sidecar is the stale case `--diff` sees most, and it falls through to rung 1 inside the sweep.
  Fix: `ls-files --cached --others --exclude-standard`, or say in the code why not.
- **m6 MINOR** `integration.sh:1315-1330`. The rerun has no cap. A change that reddens the whole
  tier (ground touched, 166 scenarios) is followed by 166 serial cold boots, about 9 min at 3.3 s
  each and more with hard timeouts, on the path where the answer is already red. Fix: skip the
  rerun past K failures (for example max(2, 10% of the slice)) and print one line saying so.
- **N7 NIT** `integration.sh:1353`. A scenario still red after its rerun is summarised from the
  SWEEP transcript. The alone transcript (`$n.alone.log`) is never shown and is deleted with `$TMP`.
- **N8 NIT**. Pre-existing seam: `scenario.sh:582` (rung 1) runs an in-tree editor pass from every
  sweep job that hits a cold cache, in parallel. Also, a touched fixture nobody loads (a deletion, a
  README under `tests/support/`) boots the whole tier. That is safe, but it costs a full sweep.
  Helpers under `<source>/support/` are neither scanned nor ground.
