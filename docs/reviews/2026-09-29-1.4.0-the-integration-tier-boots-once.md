**Verdict: HOLD.** Two warm-mode PASSes over failures the cold path reports red, both reproduced

## Blockers
- **C1 CRITICAL scenario.sh:512-531.** When every member has a verdict, the engine's exit code is
  ignored. Only a hang counts, through `killed`. Stub prints 3 PASS, then `exit 139`: cold `scenario.sh a` exits 139 (red).
  `--suite a b c` exits 0, and `integration.sh --all` in warm mode prints `4 passed, 0 failed`, rc 0 (cold: 0 passed, 4 failed, rc 1).
  Fix: a non-zero, non-hang engine exit hands back every member (the crash belongs to no slice).
- **C2 CRITICAL scenario.sh split_suite_transcript / integration.sh:1575.** Exit-time engine output
  (`WARNING: ObjectDB instances leaked at exit`, "resources still in use") lands in the LAST
  slice. Stub: a leaks. Cold, `--diff` is red on `a`. Warm, `--diff` blames `d`, reruns it cold, and prints
  `WARM-ONLY d` then `4 passed (0 flaky, 1 warm-only)`, rc 0. So the leaker goes green and the victim is named.
  Fix: engine errors after the last verdict belong to no scenario. Hand the suite back to the cold path, or fail it
  unattributed, and never let WARM-ONLY clear them. The contract should also require each scenario's teardown to
  finish before its verdict line.
- **M1 MAJOR scenario.sh:313-315 (with 378).** `tap_parse_errors` sits upstream of
  `tap_suite_progress`, and its `{ print }` to a pipe is block-buffered. The stall watch therefore sees
  progress only after about 4 KB of output, which makes the bound per-suite, not per-scenario. Stub: 4 scenarios at 2 s each,
  bound 3 s. The worker is killed at b after 4 s, and b, c and d are handed back, although no scenario took over 2 s.
  With a quiet runner and the 60 s default, every worker slice longer than 60 s is killed and goes cold, so the
  feature's saving goes with it. The failure is safe (no false PASS), but it breaks the ship criterion. Fix: `fflush()` after each print in both taps,
  plus a slow-but-progressing self-test case.
