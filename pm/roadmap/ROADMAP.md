## Before v1.0.0

This repository's public history begins at `v1.0.0`. Everything before it — twelve milestones of
work on a tool that carried both a Godot kit and a repo-discipline half — is in a private archive,
along with its tags, PRs and ledgers. Two consequences a reader should know about rather than
discover:

- **Commit hashes in `0.25.0-the-godot-kit-alone/` do not resolve here.** That milestone's records
  were written against the pre-v1.0.0 history and are kept because they are the evidence for what
  this release contains — the story closes, the review record and its eleven landed findings. The
  hashes they name live in the archive.
- **The 0.25.0 milestone directory is the record of the split itself**, not a milestone that
  shipped separately from v1.0.0. It is the work v1.0.0 is made of.
